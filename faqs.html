Generators & Sub-Stations

Very reliable. Diesel generators are built for heavy-duty use and long life, with robust construction that runs continuously under extreme conditions. Industries like manufacturing and healthcare depend on them to avoid outages. Our units meet strict standards and often include automatic controls for safe operation, and diesel engines have fewer moving parts, so they need less frequent maintenance.

We help you determine capacity based on your needs by calculating the total wattage of all critical equipment you want to power. Our team performs a site assessment and load analysis to select a generator that handles the necessary load with some buffer, so you're never over- or under-powered.

Routine maintenance is straightforward — periodic oil and filter changes, fuel system inspections, and test runs. Diesel engines have fewer moving parts than some alternatives, so they're less prone to wear. We can set up a maintenance plan, and regular load testing ensures your generator starts reliably when needed.

Our substation services are truly turnkey — we handle design, engineering, fabrication, assembly, and installation. Every substation includes all necessary components (transformers, switchgear, control panels) built to project specifications, with pre-dispatch testing and on-site support during commissioning.

Substations are the backbone of power distribution — they safely transform high-voltage grid electricity into the lower voltage used by homes, businesses, and equipment. A well-designed substation ensures stable voltage, isolates faults, and prevents outages, keeping power flowing reliably to every end user.

Solar & General

Solar systems offer clean, renewable energy and real cost savings by harnessing the sun to generate electricity, lowering utility bills and protecting you from fuel price spikes. They also reduce emissions compared to traditional power, can increase property value, and may qualify for government incentives.
